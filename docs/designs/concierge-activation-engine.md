# CEO Plan: Concierge Activation Engine — Go-to-Market for VolunteerReady

Generated by /plan-ceo-review on 2026-03-20

## Vision

### 10x Check

**"Nonprofit Activation Engine"** — Instead of passively onboarding 3 nonprofits, build a structured activation program that turns every concierge onboarding into a case study, a referral engine, and a content asset — simultaneously. Two parallel tracks: concierge onboarding (learn) + screening wedge landing page (monetize). Every touchpoint is designed to produce data, referrals, or content.

The 10x result: instead of 3 orgs and a hypothesis, you have 3 case studies with real numbers, referral leads from peak-satisfaction prompts, a landing page with social proof and a switch-cost calculator, and a repeatable "founder-led sales" motion.

### Platonic Ideal

The platonic ideal isn't a product — it's a **movement**. A volunteer coordinator at a food bank discovers VolunteerReady. In one afternoon, the founder personally sets everything up. Within a week, background checks are automated, shifts are filled, and a lapsed 3-year volunteer gets a re-engagement email and comes back. The coordinator saves 8 hours a week. She tells the coordinator at the animal shelter. He tells the Habitat chapter lead. Six months later, VolunteerReady is the tool volunteer coordinators recommend to each other — not because of marketing, but because of word-of-mouth from people whose lives got measurably better.

The feeling: **relief**. The coordinator's shoulders literally drop when they realize they don't have to track background check expirations in a spreadsheet anymore.

## Success Criteria (carried from office-hours, expanded)

- 3 nonprofits onboarded within 2-4 weeks (plan for 8-10 contacts to land 3)
- At least 1 org actively using after 30 days ("active" = at least 1 staff member logging in weekly)
- Founder can name the specific person, their workflow, and the hours saved
- Founder has a pricing hypothesis backed by at least 1 "would you pay $X?" conversation (via day-30 feedback form)
- Founder has observed at least 1 surprise — something the user did that contradicted assumptions
- Landing page receives at least 100 unique visitors in first 30 days (baseline for conversion measurement)
- At least 1 referral lead generated from concierge orgs

## If This Fails

If 0 of 3 orgs are actively using after 30 days:
1. **Revisit the ICP** — maybe volunteer coordinators aren't the right buyer (try EDs, board members, or corporate CSR managers)
2. **Revisit the wedge** — maybe the full platform is overwhelming; pivot to Approach B (screening-only at $29/mo) as primary, not just a landing page
3. **Revisit the pitch** — maybe the value prop needs reframing (compliance/risk reduction vs. time savings)
4. **Check the product** — if orgs signed up but churned, the product has UX gaps the concierge phase should have revealed

A null result is data, not failure.

## Accepted Scope — Implementation Specs

### Core: Screening Wedge Landing Page (CC: ~2 hours)
A standalone marketing page at `/screening` (or similar public route) positioned as "Background Checks for Nonprofits — $29/mo." Includes:
- Hero section with founder video embed (Loom or YouTube)
- Switch Cost Calculator (see spec below)
- Feature highlights (Checkr + Sterling integration, FCRA compliance, credential tracking)
- CTA: "Get Set Up Free by Our Founder" (links to a Calendly or contact form)
- Social proof section (initially placeholder, populated with concierge org logos + quotes after permission)
- Must follow DESIGN.md (Fraunces headings, Geist body, forest green + sand palette, shadcn components)

### Expansion 1: Before/After Impact Report (CC: ~1 hour)
**What it shows:** Usage summary at day 30 — NOT a before/after comparison (since "before" data is not in the database).
**Baseline capture at onboarding:** During concierge setup, the founder manually enters baseline data via a simple form on the org settings page: "How many volunteers do you manage?", "Hours/week spent on volunteer admin?", "How do you currently track background checks?" (free text). Stored as `Organization.onboardingBaseline` (JSON field).
**Day-30 report compares:** Baseline answers vs. actual platform usage (volunteers managed, checks completed, shifts created, credentials issued). Auto-generated as a page at `/app/impact-report`. Reuses `getApplicationFunnel(orgId, org.createdAt)` for submitted/approved counts. Requires a new `getOrgLifetimeSummary(orgId)` query for total volunteers managed, background checks completed, shifts created, and credentials issued since org creation.
**Delivery:** Email with link to the report page, triggered by a cron job checking `Organization.createdAt + 30 days`.

### Expansion 2: Switch Cost Calculator (CC: ~1 hour)
**Inputs:** Number of active volunteers (slider: 10–500), hours/week on manual tracking (slider: 1–20).
**Formula:** Annual cost = hours/week × 52 × $25/hr (avg nonprofit admin wage). Display: "You're spending ~$X/year on manual volunteer management. VolunteerReady: $29/mo ($348/year)."
**Component:** Self-contained React component using shadcn Slider + Card. No backend needed — pure client-side calculation.
**Responsive:** Must work on mobile (shadcn components are responsive by default).

### Expansion 3: "Powered by VolunteerReady" Footer (CC: ~15 min)
**Location:** Bottom of all public apply pages (`/apply/[orgSlug]`).
**Content:** "Powered by VolunteerReady — Free volunteer management for nonprofits" with link to landing page.
**Opt-out:** Controlled by `Organization.showPoweredBy` boolean (default: true). Paid/enterprise tiers can set to false. No UI for toggling yet — admin can update via DB or future settings page.
**Privacy note:** Footer is on the apply form page (collects volunteer PII). The link goes to VolunteerReady's marketing page, not a tracking pixel. No additional privacy/terms update needed — it's a static link, not data sharing.

### Expansion 4: Onboarding Checklist (CC: ~1 hour)
**Steps (4 milestones):**
1. ✅ Invite a team member (tracked: `OrganizationMember` count > 1)
2. ✅ Create your first opportunity (tracked: `Opportunity` count > 0)
3. ✅ Receive your first application (tracked: `VolunteerApplication` count > 0)
4. ✅ Complete your first background check (tracked: `BackgroundCheckRequest` with status COMPLETE or CONSIDER > 0)
**Component:** Progress bar on the dashboard (`/app`) for orgs where not all 4 steps are complete. Uses existing tRPC queries — no new backend needed, just a client component that checks counts.
**Dismissible:** "Hide checklist" button stores `Organization.onboardingComplete` boolean.
**Onboarding-activated:** Org reaches step 3 within 7 days (distinct from "monthly-active" in success criteria, which means at least 1 staff member logging in weekly after day 30).

### Expansion 5: Referral Prompt (CC: ~30 min)
**Trigger:** First `BackgroundCheckRequest` transitions to CLEAR or CONSIDER status for an org.
**UI:** Toast notification (shadcn Toast) on the screener page: "Your first background check is complete! Know another nonprofit that could use this? [Share Link]"
**Capture:** "Share Link" copies a URL: `/apply/refer?from=[orgSlug]`. The referral landing page renders the same content as `/screening` except the hero swaps in a `<ReferralBadge orgSlug={from} />` above the CTA; org display name fetched server-side from `Organization.name` by slug. Referral source tracked via URL param stored in a `referralSource` field on new `Organization` records.
**Note:** For 3 concierge orgs, a personal founder ask is more effective than an automated prompt. This feature is for the next wave of orgs after concierge. Building it now (~30-45 min including migration) avoids retrofitting later. The DB migration overhead is shared with other new fields in the same migration.

### Expansion 6: Structured Feedback Form (CC: ~45 min)
**Delivery:** Email via existing `sendEmail()` helper with link to survey page.
**Timing:** Triggered by cron checking `Organization.createdAt + 7 days` and `+ 30 days`. Idempotency: before sending, query `OrgFeedback` for an existing record with matching `orgId + type` (DAY_7 or DAY_30); skip if found. No separate timestamp field needed — the `OrgFeedback` table itself serves as the idempotency check.
**Day-7 questions:** "What's working well?", "What's confusing or broken?", "Anything you expected that's missing?"
**Day-30 questions:** Day-7 questions + "Would you pay $29/mo to keep using this? (Yes / Maybe / No)" + "Can we use your org's name and a quote on our website?"
**Storage:** `OrgFeedback` table (orgId, sentAt, responses JSON, type: DAY_7 | DAY_30).
**Relationship to concierge observation:** This supplements (not replaces) the founder's in-person observation. The form captures the org's own words in writing, which becomes quotable testimonial material.

### Expansion 7: Founder Video Intro (CC: ~0, human: ~30 min)
Record a 2-minute Loom demoing: create an org → post an opportunity → volunteer applies → run a background check. Embed on the landing page hero section. No code involved beyond the embed URL in the landing page component.
