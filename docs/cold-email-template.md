# Cold Email Template — Animal Shelter Outreach

Use this template for manual cold outreach to animal shelters. Personalize the bracketed fields before sending.

---

**Subject:** Quick question about [SHELTER_NAME]'s volunteer screening

Hi [FIRST_NAME],

I'm Jason, the founder of VolunteerReady. We built a volunteer screening platform that automates background checks, credential tracking, and compliance documentation for nonprofits.

I noticed [SHELTER_NAME] works with volunteers who handle animals — which often means insurance requirements, liability exposure, and onboarding bottlenecks that spreadsheets can't solve.

We're onboarding our first 5 animal shelters with white-glove setup, priority support, and a locked-in founding rate. I'd love to show you how it works — takes about 15 minutes.

Interested? You can:
- **See the platform:** https://volunteerready.org/for/animal-shelters?utm_source=cold-email&utm_campaign=shelter-pilot&utm_content=[SHELTER_SLUG]
- **Book a call:** https://volunteerready.org/for/animal-shelters?utm_source=cold-email&utm_campaign=shelter-pilot&utm_content=[SHELTER_SLUG]#lead-form

Either way, happy to answer any questions.

Best,
Jason

---

## Personalization guide

| Placeholder | What to put |
|---|---|
| `[SHELTER_NAME]` | Full org name (e.g., "Central Valley Animal Rescue") |
| `[FIRST_NAME]` | Contact's first name |
| `[SHELTER_SLUG]` | Kebab-case shelter identifier for UTM tracking (e.g., "central-valley-animal-rescue") |

## UTM parameters

- `utm_source=cold-email` — identifies email as the channel
- `utm_campaign=shelter-pilot` — identifies this as the shelter pilot campaign
- `utm_content=[SHELTER_SLUG]` — identifies which specific shelter was contacted

These are captured in the lead form and visible in the admin leads dashboard.
