# VolunteerReady Outreach Emails — Batch 1 (Central Valley + Sacramento animal welfare)

Rewritten from `volunteerready_outreach_cleaned.csv`. Ready-to-send drafts now live in
`volunteerready_outreach_rewritten.csv` (columns: **Tailored Subject Line**, **Tailored Email Draft**,
**Short Phone Script**). The old `Email Draft` column has been neutralized so the obsolete version
doesn't get sent by mistake.

Angle per your call: **lead with time saved**, no compliance or background-check pitch in the cold
email. Save that for the call.

## What was wrong with the originals (and is now fixed)

- **A broken template variable shipped in all 34 drafts:** "Your nonprofit page pain points line up
  closely with animal welfare teams." Removed.
- **Internal strategy notes pasted into the body** as if they were copy ("...the strongest fit may
  be: Position the app as a way to...", "...the use case I'd want to explore is: Application queue,
  auto-scored screening..."). Removed.
- **A factual overpromise:** drafts said the free plan "includes audit logging." It doesn't — Free is
  up to 3 opportunities / 3 team members, custom screening forms, scheduling + attendance, and
  portable credentials. Emails now only claim what Free actually does.
- **"Nonprofit app" pitched to government:** 13 of 34 are city/county shelters run by PDs or sheriffs.
  Those emails no longer call it a "nonprofit app" or assume a self-serve signup; they offer a free
  setup and a 15-minute conversation.
- **AI tells removed:** no em-dashes, no four-item list pile-ups, no identical openers across
  segments, no "a animal rescue" grammar errors. Length cut from 200+ words to ~90–130.
- **Two conflicting draft columns** collapsed into one.
