# Solopreneur Profile
Last updated: 2026-07-07

## Business
- Brand or name: VolunteerReady.org (distinct from VolunteerMatch.org, which was acquired by Idealist)
- One-liner: A service that matches volunteers with nonprofit organizations.
- What they sell: Free membership for volunteers; free and paid membership tiers for nonprofit organizations.
- Stage: Idea

## Audience
- Who: Two audiences — (1) volunteers looking for opportunities, and (2) nonprofit volunteer coordinators looking for qualified volunteers.
- Main problem: Helping volunteers find opportunities they're qualified for, and helping nonprofits find qualified volunteers for their opportunities.
- Hangs out on: Reddit, Facebook

## Offers
- Free: Volunteer membership (always free); free tier for nonprofit organizations
- Paid: Nonprofit organization tiers — $49/mo and $149/mo (Pro)

## Content
- Primary channel: Reddit / Facebook (no newsletter yet; Substack open as a future option)
- Other channels: Substack (potential, not yet active)
- Pillars:
  1. Volunteer opportunity matching (helping volunteers find roles they're qualified for)
  2. Nonprofit volunteer recruitment & screening
  3. Volunteer coordinator tips/pain points
  4. Nonprofit management/operations
  5. Community impact stories

## Voice
- Sounds like: Authoritative, helpful, compassionate
- Never use: Em dashes
- Point of view: First person with personal stories

## Goals
- 90-day goal: Get volunteers and nonprofits to create accounts
- Key metric: Signups
